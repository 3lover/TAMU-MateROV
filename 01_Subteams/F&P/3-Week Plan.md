## Documentation

All research and task completion should be recorded in the following structure:

- **Software module completions** — log each completed module/certificate
- **Personal research documents** — each member records their own research hours and findings
- **Team research document** — after adding to your personal doc, copy findings into the shared team doc
  - Document history will be used to verify individual contributions and prevent duplicate research on the same topic
- **Research hour logs** — ensure each member is meeting their committed hours

> **Note:** Choose specific Explorer class teams to research and divide them among team members to avoid overlap. Add assignments and deadlines to [GitHub](https://github.com/3lover/TAMU-MateROV).

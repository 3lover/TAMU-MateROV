# FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions for Team Oceanus.

## General
- **What is MATE ROV?**
  [MATE ROV](https://materovcompetition.org/) is an underwater robotics competition that challenges students to solve real-world problems while gaining career-level experience. Team Oceanus will be competing within the [Pioneer](https://materovcompetition.org/pioneer), one of the most advanced levels of this competition, which means that we must work hard, apply the systems of engineering properly and try our best if we want to show MATE ROV how great Oceanus is!
